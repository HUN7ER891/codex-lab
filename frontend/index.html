How to play

  • Click an aircraft to select it. Selected aircraft show a cyan halo.
  • Press L or use the command button to clear the aircraft to land.
  • Press H or use the command button to send it back into the hold.
  • Maintain separation: the runway becomes reserved once a clearance is issued and frees up when the aircraft vacates.
  • Complete the full three-minute shift with fewer than three strikes to win.

Tips

  • Heavy jets require more runway time and award the most points.
  • Issue clearances only when the runway status is Clear.
  • Use the log to track events, warnings, and runway availability.
