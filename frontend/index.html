Runway Rush

Keep the skies safe by sequencing arrivals onto a busy runway.

Score: 0

How to play

  • Click an aircraft to select it. Selected aircraft show a cyan halo.
  • Press L to clear a selected aircraft to land.
  • Press H to order the selected aircraft to hold.
  • Prevent mid-air collisions and land aircraft to score points.
  • You earn one strike for each collision or runway incursion without clearance. Three strikes ends the shift.

Tips

  • Stagger clearances so aircraft have separation on final approach.
  • Holding patterns keep aircraft safe while you manage the runway.
  • Use the mini heads-up display above aircraft for their current state.
